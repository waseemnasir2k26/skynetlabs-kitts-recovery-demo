Common questions

Before you book.

Is this therapy or peer support? +

Peer support, informed by clinical training. If you need a licensed therapist or psychiatrist, Jessie will say so directly and help with the next step.

Will my insurance cover this? +

Most insurers do not cover peer support, which is why rates are kept low and a flex tier exists. Sessions are paid out-of-pocket through HeyPeers.

What if I'm in crisis right now? +

Please call or text 988. This practice is non-emergency support and isn't structured for active crisis intervention.

How does the discovery session work? +

30 minutes over secure video. No commitment to continue. The whole point is to see if Jessie is the right fit for what you're navigating.

A Field Note · From the Founder

You don't have to carry the weight alone.

JK
By Majesta (Jessie) Kitts
EdD · MPH · CPRS
Photograph · forest floor, fern season

Healing is not a tidy line. The recovery I lived didn't begin in a session. It began on a hard morning, in a quiet kitchen, with a phone in my hand and someone on the other end who had been there. Years later — after the EdD, the MPH, the peer-recovery training — I built a practice around that exact phone call.

Kitts Recovery Services is a peer-led practice for individuals navigating mental health and substance-use recovery. It is held over secure video. It costs less than most clinical hours, on purpose. It exists in the space between formal therapy and a friend who hasn't been there.

If any of that resonates, the door starts at twenty dollars and thirty minutes. No script. No pressure. We meet, we talk, and we find out together if this is the right fit.
